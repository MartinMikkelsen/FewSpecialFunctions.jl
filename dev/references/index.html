Bibliography

The methods implemented in this package are taken from the following sources.

Coulomb wave functions

  • D. Gaspard, Connection formulas between Coulomb wave functions, J. Math. Phys. 59, 112104 (2018), arXiv:1804.10976.
  • NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions, Chapter 33.

Whittaker functions

  • I. J. Thompson and A. R. Barnett, Coulomb and Bessel functions of complex arguments and order, J. Comput. Phys. 64, 490 (1986), doi:10.1016/0021-9991(86)90046-X.
  • NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions, Section 13.14.

Parabolic cylinder functions

  • NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions, Chapter 12.
  • A. Gil, J. Segura, and N. M. Temme, Computing the real parabolic cylinder functions U(a,x), V(a,x), ACM Trans. Math. Softw. 32, 70 (2006), preprint.
  • Reference record for the series and asymptotic formulas.

Debye functions

  • I. I. Guseinov and B. A. Mamedov, Calculation of integer and noninteger n-dimensional Debye functions using binomial coefficients and incomplete gamma functions, Int. J. Thermophys. (2007), doi:10.1007/s10765-007-0256-1.

Fermi–Dirac integrals

  • H. M. Antia, Rational function approximations for Fermi-Dirac integrals, Astrophys. J. Suppl. Ser. 84, 101 (1993).
  • X. Aymerich-Humet, F. Serra-Mestres, and J. Millan, A generalized approximation of the Fermi–Dirac integrals, J. Appl. Phys. 54, 2850 (1983), doi:10.1063/1.332276.
  • R. Kim and M. Lundstrom, Notes on Fermi-Dirac integrals, arXiv:0811.0116.

Bose–Einstein integrals

  • T. Fukushima, Analytical computation of Bose-Einstein integral of half integer orders, −9/2, −7/2, …, and 39/2, and integer orders, 1, 2, …, and 19, by minimax rational function approximations (2020 preprint), doi:10.13140/RG.2.2.21720.65283.

Marcum Q-function

  • A. Gil, J. Segura, and N. M. Temme, Computation of the Marcum Q-function, arXiv:1311.0681.

Voigt function

  • S. M. Abrarov, B. M. Quine, and R. K. Jagpal, Rapidly convergent series for high-accuracy calculation of the Voigt function, J. Quant. Spectrosc. Radiat. Transf. 111, 372 (2010), doi:10.1016/j.jqsrt.2009.09.005.

Fresnel integrals

  • M. R. Zaghloul and L. Alrawas, Calculation of Fresnel integrals of real and complex arguments up to 28 significant digits, Numer. Algorithms (2023), doi:10.1007/s11075-023-01654-2.

Dawson integral

  • M. R. Zaghloul, Numerical calculation of Dawson's integral and its imaginary error functions of complex arguments for arbitrary value of the phase angle, Numer. Algorithms 95, 1291 (2023), doi:10.1007/s11075-023-01608-8.

Clausen functions

  • Quadrature processes for efficient calculation of the Clausen functions, BIT Numer. Math. (2023), doi:10.1007/s10543-023-00944-4.
